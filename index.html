Youtube Auto

Youtube Auto is a private tool used by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

What it does

  • Uploads and schedules short videos to the owner's channels.
  • Updates channel descriptions, keywords and banners, and posts a comment under each video.
  • Reads view and subscriber statistics of these channels for the owner's reports.

It uses Google sign-in and the YouTube APIs. Access is granted separately for each channel by its owner.
