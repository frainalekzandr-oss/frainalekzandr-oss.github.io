Youtube Auto: Privacy Policy

Youtube Auto is a private tool used by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels. Only the Google accounts of these channels sign in to it.

Data the app accesses

  • Upload access (youtube.upload): to upload and schedule videos.
  • YouTube account access (youtube.force-ssl): to read channel details, update descriptions, keywords and banners, and post comments under the channel's videos.
  • Analytics, read only (yt-analytics.readonly): to read view and subscriber statistics.

How the data is used and stored

  • Sign-in tokens are stored on the owner's server and are used only to perform the actions above.
  • Video records and statistics are stored on the same server for the owner's reports.
  • Summaries of the statistics are sent to the owner through a private Telegram bot.

Sharing

The data is not sold, is not used for advertising and is not shared with anyone else. It is exchanged with Google and YouTube to perform the actions above, and statistics summaries pass through Telegram as described above.

Youtube Auto's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google terms

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and data

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions. Tokens and stored data are deleted on request to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen of this app. The same email is used for any questions about this policy.

Коротко по-русски

Приложение заливает и планирует ролики, обновляет оформление каналов, оставляет комментарии и читает статистику только каналов владельца. Ключи входа и статистика хранятся на сервере владельца, сводки приходят ему в закрытый Telegram-бот. Данные не продаются и никому больше не передаются. Отозвать доступ можно в настройках аккаунта Google, удалить данные — по запросу на почту поддержки с экрана входа Google.
